# Changelog

## Version 13

- Added an **Independent project** clause (clause 1) to the Terms & conditions: the app is authored and developed by Henry van der Schyff and is not an official government, university, conservation-agency or institutional product; no affiliation, sponsorship or endorsement is implied. The remaining clauses were renumbered 2 to 8.

## Version 12 — 22 November 2026

- Renamed the app **Southern Africa Offline Field Log** and refreshed the index page wording and hero artwork.
- Added a hidden, offline species catalogue: 270 flora entries from the supplied South African CSV and 130 starter fauna entries.
- Added clickable species suggestions that fill the family, genus, species and available common-name fields.
- Rewrote the user manual for education and research audiences and added in-app terms and conditions.
- Removed the non-working PowerPoint report feature and its bundled library; retained PDF reports.
- Removed gallery and internet-download photo uploads from the cultural-sites form; camera capture remains.
- Added capability-aware camera/lens selection and macro focus where exposed by the device/browser, plus best-effort stabilisation controls where supported.
- Added flower, mammal and San rock-art silhouettes with section labels on tabs and section headings.
- Kept the existing IndexedDB name and schema version to preserve previously saved observations.
